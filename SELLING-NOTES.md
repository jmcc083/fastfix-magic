# Clones Tech Website — Sales / Handover Notes

This version is positioned as a polished sales demo for Clones Tech Repair.

## What changed
- Rewrote the hero around the real customer promise: fast, fair, friendly local help.
- Removed the overly corporate “restored to perfection” tone.
- Replaced “Book Your Repair” with direct call / Facebook CTAs that work without a booking backend.
- Added a sticky mobile Call / Message bar for conversions.
- Reduced risky blanket claims around 30–60 minute repairs. Customer review evidence is still used accurately.
- Reworked trust messaging around local reputation rather than an artificial review count statistic.
- Corrected the intercom testimonial so it is not incorrectly attributed to Colm O'Reilly.
- Moved repair/problem-solving ahead of product retail in the hierarchy.
- Made the product section easier to browse on mobile.
- Added current public contact details and the official Facebook page.
- Simplified LocalBusiness schema so it does not publish unverified opening hours or ratings.
- Kept the existing Clones Tech logo, shop image and product assets from the Lovable project.

The main value is not “having a website”. It is having a site that turns Google/Facebook visitors into calls and walk-ins, gives the business a professional home online, showcases customer proof, and makes Clones Tech look established before a customer ever walks through the door.
